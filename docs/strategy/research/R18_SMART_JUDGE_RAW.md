Sustaining Recall@5 as Smart Judge grows 15–25× requires combining structure-aware chunking, symmetric Persian normalization, single-engine hybrid search, bi-temporal pre-filtering, and 1-hop citation-graph expansion.Structure-Aware Chunking and Symmetric Persian NormalizationIranian statutes are hierarchically structured (قانون Law -> کتاب/باب/فصل Book/Chapter -> ماده Article -> بند Clause -> تبصره Note). Fixed token windows routinely detach a تبصره exception from its governing ماده, producing legally inverted answers. Index every short article (≤400 tokens) as a single Qdrant point; for long multi-note articles, split into a parent article chunk plus one chunk per تبصره or بند, prepending the full structural path (e.g., قانون صدور چک (اصلاحی ۱۴۰۰) › ماده ۵ مکرر › تبصره ۲) and the parent article's governing lead clause into every child chunk. Mirroring the open MIT extraction pipeline of QomSSLab/legal_laws_lite_one_by_one_v1, parse approval, notification, and effective dates, issuing authority, document type, and inline repeal badges (e.g., [نسخ‌صریح‌1402/11/21]) into indexed payload fields at ingestion.Persian normalization must run identically on indexed text and incoming user queries without mutating displayed legal quotations. Follow four deterministic rules:Dual Text Fields: Store text_display (verbatim Official Gazette spelling with authentic ZWNJ, Persian digits, and diacritics intact) for rendering and citations, alongside text_search_norm for BM25 indexing and deterministic comparison.Arabic-to-Persian Character Folding: Fold Arabic Yeh (ي U+064A) and Alef Maksura (ى) to Persian Yeh (ی U+06CC), Arabic Kaf (ك U+0643) to Persian Keheh (ک U+06A9), ة to ه, and hamza-Alef forms (أ إ ٱ) to ا (never apply this folding to Arabic-language legal quotes if any exist).Zero-Width Non-Joiner (ZWNJ, U+200C) Discipline: Keep canonical ZWNJ in text_display and dense-embedding input—می‌شود is one word, whereas substituting a space splits it and stripping merges it. Collapse repeated ZWNJs, strip stray ZWNJs next to spaces or punctuation, and normalize ZWNJ boundaries consistently in text_search_norm (either via a compact regex module or Hazm v0.12.1, MIT license, updated April 2026; earlier Persian tokenization benchmarks reported 98.75% F1 for Hazm arXiv:2202.10879 [>12 months old]).Digits, Diacritics, and Bidi Controls: Inside text_search_norm and query comparison passes only, fold Persian (۰-۹), Arabic-Indic (٠-٩), and ASCII (0-9) numerals to ASCII 0-9, and strip harakat, tashdid, tatweel (ـ), and invisible bidirectional control marks.Hybrid Search: Qdrant 1.20 Native BM25 + BGE-M3 Dense/Sparse vs. PostgresOn MIRACL Persian (fa) (632 dev queries, BAAI ACL 2024 / updated July 2024 [>12 months old]; caveat: in-domain Wikipedia, as BGE-M3 was trained on MIRACL train), BGE-M3 Dense + Sparse + Multi-Vector reaches 63.4 nDCG@10 versus 60.9 for Dense alone, 59.0 for mE5-large, 45.1 for BGE-M3 Sparse, and 28.7 for XLM-R subword BM25 (33.3 for MIRACL's Lucene baseline). Because amended articles keep most of their original wording and identical article numbers, unfiltered vector/BM25 search and cross-encoders routinely surface repealed text above current law. Prevent this by indexing integer valid_from (YYYYMMDD), valid_to (99991231 if active), status (enacted | amended | repealed | bill_unenacted), and authority_tier (1–3) on every Qdrant point so Qdrant's Filterable HNSW prunes repealed and out-of-date versions before scoring.Second, layer 1-hop local citation-graph expansion via PostgreSQL asyncpg right after Qdrant returns candidate article_ids. ### 2. What the Report Missed (12–24 Month Failure Modes)

1. **Sanctions, Geo-Blocking & Proxy Latency Penalty (Iran Context):** Groq, Google AI Studio, Deepgram, Azure Speech, OpenRouter, and Plivo/Telnyx restrict Iranian IPs and/or billing. Tunneling real-time WebSocket audio (STT/TTS) and LLM streams through a foreign gateway adds 80–250 ms RTT, packet jitter, and a single point of failure that can silently break the `<1.5 s` voice SLA.
2. **Free-Tier ToS & Sudden Account Suspension:** Building production traffic on Google AI Studio Free Tier (which trains on inputs outside EU/UK/EEA) and Groq Free Tier exposes Smart Judge to abrupt API key revocation, unannounced TPD cuts, or commercial-use ToS violations.
3. **Legal Liability & Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL):** Even at 96% citation accuracy, 4% errors on deadlines, cheque enforcement, or criminal procedure create severe legal and reputational liability. The report has no user-facing legal disclaimer architecture, no "escalate to licensed lawyer" handoff, and no immutable audit log for dispute defense.
4. **Ongoing Iranian Legal Corpus & Graph Maintenance:** The report treats scaling from 60 laws to 150K–250K points as a Week 4 ingestion job. Scraping and structuring Iranian Official Gazette (`rrk.ir`) and `qavanin.ir` amendments, PDFs, and `amends`/`supersedes` edges is a continuous data-engineering workload—stale or misparsed amendments will silently poison the bi-temporal filter.
5. **Implicit Repeals (نسخ ضمنی) & Judicial Discretion:** Bi-temporal `valid_to` tags only handle *explicit* repeals (نسخ صریح). Iranian law heavily features implicit repeals and conflicting branch interpretations that deterministic SQL/Qdrant filters cannot detect without human legal curation.
6. **Cold-Disk ColBERT `turbo4` SSD I/O & Page-Cache Thrashing:** Putting 16.9–28.2 GB of ColBERT multi-vectors on disk on a shared 8 GB VPS means random vector reads for top-30 candidates will evict PostgreSQL and ERP pages from the Linux page cache, causing severe p99 latency spikes for both Smart Judge and the ERP.
7. **Blast-Radius Coupling with the Co-Hosted ERP:** Co-locating a real-time UDP media server (LiveKit), CPU-bound ONNX inference, Qdrant, Valkey, and FastAPI on the same 4 vCPU / 8 GB machine as a production ERP means a voice traffic spike or ONNX memory leak can OOM-kill or stall core business operations.
8. **PII Leakage & Client Confidentiality:** Legal callers disclose national IDs (کد ملی), names, case numbers, and sensitive disputes. Sending unredacted prompts/transcripts to foreign free-tier APIs (especially Google AI Studio Free Tier) violates client confidentiality; the report mentions scrubbing PII in one table cell but designs no actual Persian PII redaction pipeline.
9. **Multi-Turn Fact-Gathering & Context Drift:** Real legal consultations span 5–15 turns where users drip-feed critical facts (e.g., "the cheque was post-dated"). The report optimizes single-turn Q&A and caches; without structured state tracking and multi-turn query condensation, retrieval accuracy degrades sharply after Turn 2.
10. **8 kHz PSTN Codec & Dialect WER Cascade:** Clean FLEURS benchmarks do not reflect 8 kHz G.711 Iranian mobile/landline audio with background noise and regional accents. A 25–35% real-world phone WER will break legal entity recognition (`چک صیادی`, article numbers) before retrieval even runs.
11. **Model Churn & Calibrated Threshold Drift:** Relying on preview/fast-moving models means silent vendor updates or deprecations every 3–6 months will shift output formatting and invalidate calibrated abstention score floors and NLI thresholds.
12. **Call Audio & Trace Retention / Disk Exhaustion:** Archiving voice recordings and full retrieval traces on the 24 GB offline box without TTLs, encryption at rest, or automated backups creates a compliance risk and eventual disk-full outage.
