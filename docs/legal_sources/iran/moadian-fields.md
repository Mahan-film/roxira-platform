This document is compiled **exclusively** from the two local source files in `docs/legal_sources/iran/`:

1. **`e-invoice-instruction-tir-1405.txt`** — *دستورالعمل صدور صورتحساب الکترونیکی* (`RC_IITP.IS_V7.9`, Tir 1405, 116 pages).
2. **`moadian-technical-connection-14010422.txt`** — *دستورالعمل فنی نحوه اتصال به سامانه مودیان* (`RC_TICS.IS_V01`, Khordad 1401, 69 pages).

## 1. Legend for Invoice Types & Templates (`inty` / `inp`)

In `e-invoice-instruction-tir-1405.txt` (Section 4, Table 1, p. 14; Section 8-3, Table 6, p. 28; Section 8-6, Table 9, p. 31), field requirement status is defined across **16 combinations** of Invoice Type (`inty`) and Invoice Template (`inp`):

- **Type 1 (`inty = 1`, صورتحساب الکترونیکی نوع اول) — 12 Templates:**
  - **`T1-P1`**: الگوی اول (فروش)
  - **`T1-P2`**: الگوی دوم (فروش ارز) *(Note: in Khordad 1401 Table 7 p. 22, `inp = 2` was titled «فروش ارزی» and covered export/customs fields)*
  - **`T1-P3`**: الگوی سوم (صورتحساب طلا، جواهر و پلاتین)
  - **`T1-P4`**: الگوی چهارم (قرارداد پیمانکاری)
  - **`T1-P5`**: الگوی پنجم (قبوض خدماتی)
  - **`T1-P6`**: الگوی ششم (بلیط هواپیما)
  - **`T1-P7`**: الگوی هفتم (صادرات)
  - **`T1-P8`**: الگوی هشتم (بارنامه)
  - **`T1-P9`**: الگوی نهم (فروش فرآورده‌های نفتی — پالایش و پخش)
  - **`T1-P11`**: الگوی یازدهم (بورس اوراق بهادار مبتنی بر کالا)
  - **`T1-P13`**: الگوی سیزدهم (فروش خدمات بیمه‌ای)
  - **`T1-P14`**: الگوی چهاردهم (فروش زنجیره)
  *(Templates 10 and 12 are not present in Table 1 or Table 9 of Tir 1405 — `unclear` why numbers 10 and 12 are skipped.)*
- **Type 2 (`inty = 2`, صورتحساب الکترونیکی نوع دوم) — 4 Templates:**
  - **`T2-P1`**: الگوی اول (فروش)
  - **`T2-P3`**: الگوی سوم (صورتحساب طلا، جواهر و پلاتین)
  - **`T2-P9`**: الگوی نهم (فروش فرآورده‌های نفتی — پالایش و پخش)
  - **`T2-P13`**: الگوی سیزدهم (فروش خدمات بیمه‌ای)
- **Type 3 (`inty = 3`, صورتحساب الکترونیکی نوع سوم):**
  - Defined in Tir 1405 Section 3-3 (p. 12) as a bank POS / payment gateway receipt containing only payment amount, acceptor details (switch number, merchant acceptor number, terminal number), tracking number, payment date/time, minimum seller details, and `taxid`. Table 6 (p. 28) states `inty` only accepts values `1` or `2` in submitted e-invoice JSON; Type 3 is not a column in Table 1 (p. 14).
- **Requirement Status Codes in Tir 1405 Table 1 (p. 14):**
  - **`اجباری` (Mandatory)**: Value must be provided in the template.
  - **`اختیاری` (Optional)**: Presence or absence of a value does not prevent invoice issuance.
  - **`در شرایط خاص اجباری` (Conditionally Mandatory)**: Mandatory when specific rules/conditions hold (detailed in the Validation Rule column).
  - **`ignore` / `خارج از الگو` (Out of Pattern)**: Not applicable to the template; if sent, it is ignored/not received by the system (or rejected per Section 8 rules where specified).

## 3. Disagreements Between Khordad 1401 Technical Document (`RC_TICS.IS_V01`) and Tir 1405 Instruction (`RC_IITP.IS_V7.9`)

Every substantive difference between `moadian-technical-connection-14010422.txt` (Khordad 1401, `RC_TICS.IS_V01`) and `e-invoice-instruction-tir-1405.txt` (Tir 1405, `RC_IITP.IS_V7.9`) identified from the text of both sources is listed below with citations.

### 3.1 Total Count of Invoice Fields and Field Removals / Additions
* **1401 (`RC_TICS.IS_V01`, Sec. 7-3-1-2 Table 7, pp. 21–26):** Defines **67 invoice fields** (Header: 33 fields [rows 1–31, 33, plus row 32 `dpvb`], Body: 27 fields [rows 35–61], Payment: 7 fields [rows 62–67, with row 67 duplicated for `pid` and `pdt`]).
* **Tir 1405 (`RC_IITP.IS_V7.9`, Sec. 7 Table 2, pp. 19–22):** Defines **102 invoice fields** (Header: 46 fields, Body: 47 fields, Payment: 9 fields).
* **Removed field:** `dpvb` (عدم پرداخت مالیات بر ارزش افزوده خریدار — row 32 in 1401 Table 7, p. 24) is completely removed in Tir 1405.
* **Added fields (36 new fields):**
  * **Header (27 new fields):** `cdcn` (row 20), `cdcd` (row 21), `tonw` (row 30), `torv` (row 31), `tocv` (row 32), `tinc` (row 78), `lno` (row 81), `lrno` (row 82), `ocu` (row 83), `oci` (row 84), `dco` (row 85), `dci` (row 86), `tid` (row 87), `rid` (row 88), `lt` (row 89), `cno` (row 90), `did` (row 91), `sg` (row 92), `sgid` (row 93), `sgt` (row 94), `asn` (row 95), `asd` (row 96), `in` (row 97), `an` (row 98), `insr` (row 100), `nti1` (row 101), `nti2` (row 102).
  * **Body (7 new fields):** `nw` (row 42), `ssrv` (row 47), `sscv` (row 48), `cui` (row 77), `cpr` (row 79), `sovat` (row 80), `vba` (row 99).
  * **Payment (2 new fields):** `pmt` (row 71), `pv` (row 76).

### 3.2 Invoice Templates (`inp`) Classification and Scope
* **1401 (Table 7 row 7, p. 22):** Defines **6 templates** (`1..6`):
  1. الگوی اول: فروش
  2. الگوی دوم: فروش ارزی
  3. الگوی سوم: طلا، جواهر و پلاتین
  4. الگوی چهارم: قرارداد پیمانکاری
  5. الگوی پنجم: قبوض خدماتی
  6. الگوی ششم: بلیط هواپیما
  * Length is specified as `1`.
  * In 1401, Template 2 was titled «فروش ارزی» and contained customs export fields `scln` (شماره پروانه گمرکی) and `scc` (کد گمرک محل اظهار فروشنده).
* **Tir 1405 (Table 1, p. 14; Sec. 8-6 Table 9, pp. 31–32):** Defines **12 templates** across numbers `1` through `14` (templates 10 and 12 are skipped/unassigned):
  * Length is updated to `حداکثر ۲` (`<= 2`).
  * Template 2 is fundamentally redefined from general foreign exchange/export to «فروش ارز» (currency exchange sales by licensed banks and exchange bureaus, with new fields `cpr` [نرخ خرید ارز] and `sovat` [ماخذ مالیات بر ارزش افزوده]).
  * Export sales with customs declaration are moved to a distinct dedicated template: **Template 7** («صادرات»), accompanied by customs clearance fields `scc`, `scln`, `cdcn`, `cdcd`, `tonw`, `torv`, `tocv`, `nw`, `ssrv`, `sscv`.
  * Additional new templates: Template 8 (بارنامه), Template 9 (فروش فرآورده‌های نفتی — پالایش و پخش), Template 11 (بورس اوراق بهادار مبتنی بر کالا), Template 13 (فروش خدمات بیمه‌ای), Template 14 (فروش زنجیره).

### 3.3 Field `indati2m` Purpose, Persian Title, Timing Rule, and Requirement
* **1401 (Table 7 row 3, p. 22; Table 14 `01302`, p. 34):**
  * Persian title: «تاریخ و زمان ایجاد صورتحساب (میلادی)».
  * Length: `14` (`Timestamp`).
  * Description: Creation timestamp of invoice; rule states «حداکثر تا تاریخ صدور صورتحساب» ($\text{indati2m} \le \text{indatim}$).
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 6, p. 18; Table 1 row 3, p. 14; Table 2 row 3, p. 19; Sec. 8-2-1 Table 5, pp. 27–28):**
  * Persian title: «تاریخ و زمان ثبت صورتحساب (میلادی)».
  * Length: `حداکثر تعداد رقم صحیح ۱۳، حداکثر تعداد رقم اعشار ۰` (`عددی`).
  * Purpose & Requirement: Dedicated exclusively to Article 9 late-submitted invoices (`موضوع ماده ۹ قانون پایانه‌های فروشگاهی و سامانه مودیان`). Requirement status is `در شرایط خاص اجباری`: mandatory if and only if invoice is submitted past the legal deadline (`insr = 1`); if sent within the normal legal deadline, `indati2m` is out of pattern (`خارج از الگو` / rejected).
  * Timing Rule: Strictly inverted compared to 1401 — $\text{indati2m}$ must be strictly greater than $\text{indatim}$ ($\text{indati2m} > \text{indatim}$).

### 3.4 Timestamp Representation & Precision (`indatim`, `pdt`)
* **1401 (Table 7 rows 2 & 67, pp. 22, 26):** Data type listed as `Timestamp`, length `14` (representing 14 digits, typically `YYYYMMDDHHmmss` or a 14-digit integer).
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-2 Table 4, p. 25; Sec. 8-59 Table 61, p. 77):** Data type defined as `عددی`, length `تعداد رقم صحیح ۱۳، تعداد رقم اعشار ۰` (standard 13-digit Unix epoch time in milliseconds; Table 61 rule 1 specifies recording `pdt` with day precision). In Section 9-3 JSON sample (p. 97), both are written as `Timestamp`.

### 3.5 Buyer Classification (`tob`) Options
* **1401 (Table 7 row 10, p. 23):** Defines **5 buyer types**:
  * `1`: حقیقی
  * `2`: حقوقی
  * `3`: مشارکت مدنی
  * `4`: اتباع غیرایرانی
  * `5`: مصرف‌کننده نهایی
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-9 Table 11 rule 3, p. 34):** Defines **4 buyer types**:
  * `1`: حقیقی
  * `2`: حقوقی
  * `3`: مشارکت مدنی
  * `4`: اتباع غیرایرانی
  * Option `5` («مصرف‌کننده نهایی») is entirely omitted.

### 3.6 Data Typing of Numeric Identifiers and Codes
* **1401 (Table 7, pp. 21–26):** Defines nearly all identification, tax, registration, terminal, and tracking codes as data type `int`:
  * `tins` (`int`, length `10`)
  * `tinb` (`int`, length `10`)
  * `bid` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `sbc` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `bpc` (`int`, length `10`)
  * `bbc` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `scln` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `scc` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `crn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `billid` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `sstid` (`int`, length `13`)
  * `mu` (`int`, length `3`)
  * `bsrn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `iinn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `acn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `trmn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `trn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `pcn` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  * `pid` (`int`, length `متغیر`)
  *(Note: 1401 Section 6-2-2 sample JSON on pp. 11–13 contradicted Table 7 by putting strings in quotes, e.g. `"tins": "10320499298"`, and formatted `pcn` with dashes `"6037-9972-9856-9865"`).*
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8 Tables 11, 13, 14, 29, 32, 52, 54, 55, 56, 58, 59, 60, and Sec. 9-3, p. 97):** Formally defines all of the above code and identifier fields as `رشته عددی` (`string` of digits), preserving leading zeros and matching official identification specifications.

### 3.7 Length of Taxpayer Economic Codes (`tins`, `tinb`)
* **1401 (Table 7 rows 9 & 12, pp. 22–23):** Defines fixed length `10` (`int`) for both seller economic code `tins` and buyer economic code `tinb`. (Contradicted by 1401 JSON example p. 12 which used a 14-digit value `"32652362589632"`).
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-8 Table 11, p. 33; Sec. 8-9 Table 11, pp. 33–34):** Length is specified as `۱۱ و ۱۴ حسب مورد` (11 digits for registered legal entities, 14 digits for natural persons or partnerships).

### 3.8 Buyer National ID (`bid`) Length
* **1401 (Table 7 row 11, p. 23):** Length is specified as `متغیر`.
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-9 Table 11, p. 33):** Length is strictly restricted to `۱۰ / ۱۱ / ۱۲` (10 digits for national ID of Iranian individuals, 11 digits for national ID of Iranian legal entities, 12 digits for civil partnership ID or foreign national ID).

### 3.9 Branch Codes (`sbc`, `bbc`) and Buyer Passport Number (`bpn`) Length
* **1401 (Table 7 rows 13, 15, 17, p. 23):** Length for `sbc`, `bbc`, and `bpn` is listed as `متغیر`.
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8 Tables 11 & 12, pp. 33, 36):** `sbc` and `bbc` are fixed `۴` characters (`رشته عددی`); `bpn` is fixed `۹` characters (`رشته`).

### 3.10 Item Quantity / Amount (`am`) Data Type and Decimal Precision
* **1401 (Table 7 row 37, p. 24):** Data type is `int`, length `متغیر`. Fractional quantities were disallowed by definition.
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-29 Table 31, p. 51):** Data type is `اعشاری` (`double`), length `حداکثر تعداد رقم صحیح ۱۸، حداکثر تعداد رقم اعشار ۸`, fully accommodating fractional and weighted item quantities.

### 3.11 Unit of Measure (`mu`) Length
* **1401 (Table 7 row 38, p. 24):** Data type is `int`, length `3`.
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 8-30 Table 32, p. 51):** Data type is `رشته عددی`, length `حداکثر ۸` (`<= 8`), referencing the comprehensive coding table in Section 9-1 (Table 89, p. 96).

### 3.12 Decimal Precision Rules for Monetary Amounts and Rates
* **1401 (Table 7, pp. 21–26):** All monetary amounts and tax/fee rates (`fee`, `cfee`, `exr`, `prdis`, `dis`, `adis`, `vra`, `vam`, `odr`, `odam`, `olr`, `olam`, `consfee`, `spro`, `bros`, `tcpbs`, `cop`, `vop`, `tsstam`, `tprdis`, `tdis`, `tadis`, `tvam`, `todam`, `tbill`, `cap`, `insp`, `tvop`, `Tax17`) are generically typed as `double` with length `متغیر`.
* **Tir 1405 (Sec. 7 p. 23 rule 1; Sec. 8 Tables 15–28, 34–53):** Explicit integer and decimal digit boundaries and truncations are established for every numeric field:
  * **Rial monetary amounts** (`tprdis`, `tdis`, `tadis`, `tvam`, `todam`, `tbill`, `cap`, `insp`, `tvop`, `tax17`, `prdis`, `dis`, `adis`, `vam`, `odam`, `olam`, `consfee`, `spro`, `bros`, `tcpbs`, `cop`, `vop`, `tsstam`, `pv`, `sovat`): maximum 18 integer digits, **0 decimal digits** (calculated with truncation to exact integer Rials).
  * **Unit price and exchange rates** (`fee`, `exr`, `cpr`): maximum 18 integer digits, **8 decimal digits**.
  * **Foreign currency amounts** (`cfee`, `tocv`, `sscv`): maximum 14 integer digits, **4 decimal digits**.
  * **Tax / duty / fee rates** (`vra`, `odr`, `olr`): maximum 3 integer digits, **2 decimal digits** ($0 \le \text{rate} \le 100$).
  * **VAT Rate representation (`vra`):** In 1401 sample JSON (p. 12), `vra` was expressed as a decimal ratio `0.09`. In Tir 1405 Table 43 (p. 60), `vra` is defined as a percentage ($0..100$, e.g. `10.00`) and the calculation formula explicitly divides by 100: $K_s = I_s \times J / 100$.

### 3.13 Third-Party Contract Number (`bsrn`) Title and Legal Scope
* **1401 (Table 7 row 60, p. 25):** Titled «شناسه یکتای ثبت قرارداد حق‌العملکاری» (limited strictly to commission/brokerage agency contracts).
* **Tir 1405 (Table 2 row 66, p. 21; Sec. 8-50 Table 52, p. 71):** Renamed to «شماره قرارداد معامله از طریق شخص ثالث» (broadened to cover all third-party mediated commercial transactions registered in the taxpayer portal).

### 3.14 Case Sensitivity and Naming of Article 17 Withholding Tax
* **1401 (Table 7 row 33, p. 24; Sec. 6-2-2 JSON p. 12):** Spelled with a capital `T`: `Tax17`.
* **Tir 1405 (Table 2 row 37, p. 20; Sec. 9-3 JSON p. 97):** Spelled in all-lowercase: `tax17`.

### 3.15 Field Requirement Status Discrepancies between Table 1 and Section 8 in Tir 1405
In several instances within `e-invoice-instruction-tir-1405.txt` itself, internal differences exist between Table 1 (the general matrix on p. 14) and the specific control tables in Section 8:
* **`tdis` (مجموع تخفیفات):** Table 1 row 25 (p. 14) lists `tdis` as `اختیاری` in all applicable templates, whereas 1401 Table 14 (error `3301`, p. 35) treated it as mandatory.
* **`odt` / `odr` / `odam` and `olt` / `olr` / `olam`:** Table 1 rows 54–59 (p. 14) classify these fields as `در شرایط خاص اجباری`, whereas Section 8-43 Table 45 (p. 63) writes `اختیاری` in the header column while its rule 1 makes all three sub-fields mutually mandatory whenever any one of them is provided.
* **`pv` (مبلغ پرداختی):** Table 62 (p. 78) rule 2 states $\text{pv} < \text{tbill}$ (strictly less than total bill), while rule 5 in the exact same table states $\text{pv} \le \text{tbill}$ (less than or equal to total bill). Marked as `unclear` in Section 2.3.
