When the lawyer said "free subscription for lawyers is not a legal problem", I implemented free/trial VIP for lawyers. Owner (2026-10-13) was upset: lawyers must PAY like everyone; only lawyers who work for/with us get access, granted by the owner. The lawyer answers legal permissibility, not business policy.

**Why:** owner felt their opinion was ignored ("you act however you want"); pricing and who gets free access are core business decisions.

**How to apply:** treat lawyer/agent answers as constraints, not decisions. Before changing pricing, free tiers, discounts, access grants, or anything revenue-affecting, ask the owner explicitly (one short question). Default: no free access.
