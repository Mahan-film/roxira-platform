R17 research (stage 1 + 2) received 2026-11-17. Kept out of the public repo (personal data, sanctions).

Recommendation (both stages agree): **Option D1** — a German GmbH (25k, 12.5k paid in) or a UG funded with at least 3–5k EUR (never 1 EUR: §5a(4) GmbHG illiquidity duty), 100% owned by the Malaysian Sdn Bhd, disclosed openly in the Transparenzregister and bank KYC; the German partner is Geschäftsführer with salary/cash bonus (no equity at first), German-only bespoke Satzung with a Zustimmungskatalog, plus a backup signatory (Prokurist or second director) because a sole GF can resign instantly and founders may not travel. A hidden nominee (Einzelunternehmen in the partner's name with founders controlling) is a Strohmann risk — rejected. Fallback if 3 German banks refuse: genuine independent distributor owned by the partner (D2), never a nominee.

Facts worth keeping (verified in stage 2 against primary texts): HRegGebV 2100 = 225 EUR; TrGebV 19.80 EUR/yr; ELSTER developer access free but no legal entitlement; DATEV first data service 1,500 EUR net + 210 EUR/commenced hour, and once an app goes to Production no API products can be added and no downgrade; DATEV Premium Partner needs a security certificate (8–40k EUR, 6–18 months); §147(3) AO 10 yrs books / 8 yrs vouchers; §5a(5) GmbHG: UG limits end only after a formal capital increase; Stripe's own policy bans Iranian citizens and residents; Malaysia is not in the Apostille convention (4-step legalisation via the German embassy KL).

Product consequences (go into rows): EU AI Act Art. 50 applies from day 1 for a product launched after 2 Aug 2026 (no 2 Dec 2026 grace) → T191 before any DE launch; non-EU staff must not see unmasked German customer data (GDPR Chapter V) → support access with masking; SEPA bank collection, no US card gateways for DE; DATEV app scope must be locked before Production.

**How to apply:** owner decided legal/company steps come after the product is complete; do not push company setup now. Related: [[project-product-first-2026-11-16]], [[feedback-no-hosting-location-topic]].
