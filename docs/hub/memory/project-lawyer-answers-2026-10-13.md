Lawyer answers (sheet docs/legal/LAWYER_REVIEW_GATE_FA.md, items 1-9; item 10 unanswered):
1. ekhtebar as source: accepted (label as unofficial).
2. Implicitly repealed rules: keep, show a warning.
3. Old appeal/cassation thresholds (3M / 20M rials, arts 331/367) changed with the peace-court law: claims above 100M toman (1B rials) are for the general civil court.
4. Use the current peace-court ceiling (1B rials = 100M toman).
5. Rent laws 1356/1362/1376: each governs places within its own scope; choosing the governing law is a legal judgment → system must present all applicable regimes with their conditions, not pick one.
6. 110-coin mahr limit currently governs, and only for imprisonment: if 110 coins are paid there is no prison for the rest; unpaid installments up to 110 keep imprisonment.
7. Social security art. 76: lawyer has no definite answer → keep needs_review + disclaimer.
8. Lawyer verification: check on the bar association (کانون) website; free subscription for lawyers is fine.
9. Privacy/terms: lawyer reviews only ambiguous items.

Owner vision (2026-10-13): reduce dependence on lawyers to essential cases; full coverage of valid laws, cabinet resolutions, circulars and licensing rules (CBI, MIMT, national licensing portal, asnaf).
