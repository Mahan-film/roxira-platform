Subject: Request for permission to reuse the Laws of Malaysia (lom.agc.gov.my) in a legal research service

Dear Sir/Madam,

[Company name] Sdn. Bhd. ([registration no.]) is developing an online legal research tool for advocates and
solicitors in Malaysia. The tool helps lawyers find the relevant provisions of federal Acts and always shows
the exact section, a link to the official text on the Laws of Malaysia portal, and the date of the version used.

We would like to request permission to:
1. download the federal Acts published on the Laws of Malaysia portal, including revised reprints;
2. store them and index them section by section in our service;
3. display the text of sections to our subscribers, with clear attribution to the Attorney General's
   Chambers of Malaysia and a statement that the authentic text is the official publication;
4. provide this as part of a paid subscription service.

We will not alter the wording of any provision, will not claim endorsement by the Government, and will
update or remove texts on request. If a licence, fee or specific attribution wording is required, we would
be grateful for your guidance.

Thank you for your consideration.
