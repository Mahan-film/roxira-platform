## Operating model
- Antigravity (Gemini) builds in 4 parallel lanes.
  - L1: fixes.
  - L2: ledger, HR, menus.
  - L3: tax, cheques, electronic books.
  - L4: inventory, sales, currency, access, data.
- The queue is the newest `## WAVE` comment on issue 592.
  - WAVE 6 = fix review blockers.
  - The WAVE 5 lanes table is in the comment "WAVE 5 lanes".
- The owner relays Antigravity reports to the coordinator.
- The coordinator runs `python3 scripts/dev/verify_agent_report.py --since <date> <report>`.
- Then the coordinator does the final review.
- Always-on Antigravity rules: `.agents/rules/roxira-always.md`.
- Lessons (25 rules): `docs/agents/LESSONS.md`.
- The PR template has an Agent evidence section.

## Hourly review desk

- Critical PRs get Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max 3 per run).
