## Behaviour

1. Fiscal years for tenant A: Y1 = 2026-03-21..2027-03-20, Y2 = 2027-03-21..2028-03-20. Post (insert as POSTED, balanced two-line) three vouchers dated 2026-05-01, 2026-06-01, 2026-07-01: permanent numbers are exactly 1, 2, 3 with `permanent_fiscal_year_id` = Y1's id. A voucher dated 2027-04-01 gets number 1 of Y2 (counter is per year).
2. A DRAFT voucher has `permanent_number` NULL; moving it DRAFT -> POSTED gives it the next number of its year (4 after the three above); a DRAFT -> TEMPORARY -> POSTED path gives the next one (5).
3. Rollback: inside a transaction post a voucher (would get 6), then throw so it rolls back; the next committed POSTED voucher gets 6, not 7 (no gap).
4. Concurrency: 10 parallel transactions each post one voucher dated in Y1 (own connections); after all commit the permanent numbers of those 10 are exactly {6..15} with no duplicate and no gap.
5. Tenant B (no fiscal year row): its first POSTED voucher gets number 1 with `permanent_fiscal_year_id` 0; tenant B cannot read tenant A's counter rows.
6. Immutability: an UPDATE of `permanent_number` on a numbered voucher is refused; existing row created before the migration (insert a POSTED row with the trigger disabled, or one that existed before the migration step) keeps NULL (no backfill).
7. A vouchers reversing another (reverse path) gets its own next number.
